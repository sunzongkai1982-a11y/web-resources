The Importance of the Lunar Cycle

The lunar cycle, which spans approximately 29.5 days, is divided into eight phases: New Moon, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full Moon, Waning Gibbous, Last Quarter, and Waning Crescent. Each phase brings its own unique energy and can influence your daily horoscope. For Aries, understanding these phases can help you navigate your emotions and decisions more effectively.
