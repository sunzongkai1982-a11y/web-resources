The Dropped Pill Problem, and Why Caffeine Is in the Same Category

Call a vet or an animal poison helpline immediately. Do not wait for signs and do not induce vomiting on your own initiative — for a number of these that makes things worse. Everything below is about recognising exposure, not managing it.

Most accidental poisonings are mundane. Nobody opened a bottle and fed it to their dog; a tablet rolled off a counter, fell out of a pill organiser, or was dropped by someone who assumed nothing would come of it. The scale is small and the animal is small, and that gap is the whole problem.

Dose per kilogram is the only number that matters

The same 81 mg tablet represents a meaningfully different exposure in a 4 kg cat than in a 35 kg retriever. This is why the clinic's first questions are weight and quantity, and why "it was only half a tablet" carries no information on its own. If you need to work it through before calling, the aspirin tablet toxicity calculator does exactly that arithmetic.

Heart and blood pressure medication

Often overlooked because these are maintenance drugs rather than dramatic ones, and because the person taking them is likely to have a bottle open on a bedside table daily.

They are potent at low doses and, for some formulations, long-acting — which means effects accumulate rather than peaking and passing. Details for these cases are in the heart and blood pressure medication notes.

Hormonal contraceptives

Another underestimatted one. Combined pills contain oestrogen and progestogen, and because the strength is deliberately low by human standards, people assume the quantity is trivial.Animal diverge here in ways that matter for hormonal pill exposure, so species identity is worth stating clearly when you call.

Caffeine, which nobody files as a drug

Spilt coffee grounds, energy drinks, tea bags from a compost caddy, diet products. The reason this one catches people out is categorisation — caffeine is filed mentally as food. It is a stimulant with a narrow margin, and it is absorbed quickly.

Coffee behaves differently from energy drink, which behaves differently from concentrated powder or tablets, so knowing the source changes the answer materially. The caffeine calculator handles the different sources by type, which is the useful way to work it out rather than guessing.

The four facts, again

Exact product and strength in mg, roughly how many units are missing rather than an estimate, current body weight, and time since — plus anything else swallowed. Count what is left in the packet rather than recalling it; people consistently under-report how many they think went missing.
